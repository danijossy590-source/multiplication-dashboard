Our purpose

Why the Camp of Gilgal Exists

Over the years, we have identified various gaps in modern discipleship systems. Aside from seeing people who claim various forms of spiritual parenthood yet are not strongly established in the Word, we have also seen people who are very knowledgeable yet lack deep fellowship with God and practical application of that knowledge—resulting in them merely being puffed up by knowledge.

All our lessons on this platform are channeled toward addressing those gaps. They are completely free and designed for your spiritual growth—however, they will demand your utmost attention and require you to make them a priority.

Your learning journey

Guidelines for Every Lesson

  • Treat Lessons as a Live Classroom

    Do not treat this platform as casual online reading. Whenever you come online, eliminate distractions and focus on what you are learning as if you were sitting in a physical classroom.

  • Pray After Every Lesson

    After completing each lesson, pray in tongues for at least 10 minutes, alongside any other prayer the Holy Spirit lays on your heart.

  • Complete All Challenges

    Attempt all the challenges provided. If you fail any challenge, go back and review the lesson again until you master it.

  • Download Video & PDF Resources

    We also provide a dedicated section for video lessons and PDFs. Download them, watch, listen, and read—they are all targeted toward your spiritual growth.

  • Participate Actively in Your Pod

    You will be placed into different pods and assigned a leader. Be active in your group, ask questions, make contributions, and build godly relationships—all for the sake of mutual growth.

With you in faith

Our Commitment to You

We promise you that divine encounters await you as you read through every lesson. In all, here in the Camp of Gilgal, your spiritual growth is our responsibility. We love you and cannot wait to see you becoming all that God has purposed for you.
